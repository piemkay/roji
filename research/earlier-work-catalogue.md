# Earlier Roji and Sift work: a catalogue

Research for [#53](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/53) on the map [#43](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/43). Compiled 24 November 2026.

This is a record of what the earlier plans decided and the reasons they gave, with sources. It does not judge or recommend. Read it only after forming your own view, as a checklist of things not to miss (see the map's notes).

## How to read it

- **Status words** are the sources' own. *Decided* means the source says the user decided it, cites an accepted ADR, or a closed issue or committed file states it as a rule. *Proposed* means a recommendation or a design that was never confirmed. *Option* means one of several alternatives that were shown side by side.
- **Reasons** are quoted or closely paraphrased from the source. When a source gives no reason, the entry says so.
- **ADR, Q, F, I and experiment IDs** (ADR-0042, Q12, F121, I-041, OR-10 and so on) belong to the earlier Sift project repository, which is not in this repo. They appear here only as the artifacts cite them; the underlying documents were not read.
- **Names changed over time.** "Sift" was the working name from 9 to 19 October 2026; "Roji" from 20 October. The desktop focus companion was first called "the Bud" and later "Ishi". The people in the prototypes ("Alex", "Sam") are invented stand-ins.
- The closed issues #2 to #37 and #39 were all closed with the same comment: "planning restarts from the product without a chosen stack (#41). This issue was written for the old approach and stays here for reference." None was implemented.

## How the framing moved

A neutral timeline, because the same idea often appears under different framings.

1. **10 Oct, CASE:** Sift is "a capture and librarian app". Thesis: "Get it out of your head in seconds. Sift sorts it with you, and brings it back when you can act on it." Windows and Android, German and English, the "assistant window" (direction D) chosen in discovery.
2. **11–15 Oct, UIP:** UI rounds move from panel, thread, palette and desk towards notes, pages built from sources and drawings. Round 9 is "Sift with everything decided so far".
3. **15 Oct, TODO:** a "to-do-first" second version, built from the user's sessions and his Notion, Telegram and Notepad data.
4. **16–19 Oct, CAP, PROTO, TASK, MOCK, BUD, LISTS, GOALS, DONE:** design rounds on capture, follow-through, the task view, lists, goals, done moments and the focus companion.
5. **17–18 Oct, ARCH:** architecture, with decisions on hosting, sign-in, offline copies, models and the agentic build process.
6. **20 Oct, PLAN, W1, README, #1–#39:** renamed Roji; milestones W1 to AP; 37 W1 issues filed; the scaffold merged (#38); design tokens added.
7. **24 Nov, #41:** the scaffold is removed and planning restarts without a chosen stack; the tokens stay.

## Product framing and scope

- **Problem statement.** "Capturing is easy. Organizing afterwards is a second job that never happens." Reason: information lands wherever is fastest (Notepad, Telegram, Chrome, Notion), finding it again is slow, and rare duties slip. The source cites measurements of the user's data on 10 October 2026: 852 Notion pages with 43% of notes never filed, 52 Notepad tabs, 197 Chrome tabs and 268 bookmarks, 94 Telegram notes to himself, and about 15 Notepad tabs holding real secrets. "Methods like PARA make the user the librarian, and that is the job that doesn't get done." [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Success criteria S1–S7**, in the user's words: information organized without doing the organizing; relevant information shown going into a meeting; an overview of every project; a lean dashboard of what to do next; notifications that don't annoy and no long lists ("Having a too long list just feels demotivating."); duties don't slip; "out of his head". [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Design principles (10 Oct, from research):** one capture point and no decisions at capture time; the user's words stay as typed; "Claude proposes and you confirm. Nothing is filed silently."; few prompts at moments you can act; duties run book → appointment → done → next due; no red piles, streaks or guilt, and old items expire quietly; "It keeps working when you ignore it for a week."; rules and code first, the model only where it adds value. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Audience (20 Oct).** "A calm to-do app for individuals, households and teams." "Self-hosted or hosted: one deployment can serve many separate spaces." [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md)
- **For you first, product later (10 Oct, proposed).** "Build for you first. Keep the commercial option open with the licence and a distinctive name." Left as "your call". [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Name (proposed).** "'Sift' looks crowded"; pick a distinctive name and register a trademark before publishing. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz). The final name was still open as Q96 on 18 October. [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Notes and pages dropped from scope (decided by 20 Oct).** The plan leaves out "parked notes, pages and canvas features" (ADR-0055). [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). PROTO states "Notes stay in Notion for now." [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71)
- **Start fresh without Notion (decided).** The one-time Notion import (F106) was "Dropped by your answer to Q100: we start fresh." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A), [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY). Earlier, CASE had planned Notion and Notepad import in the Windows MVP. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Languages.** German and English, in the interface and in content. Reason: house tasks are German and work tasks English. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), [#17](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/17)
- **Market reading (10 Oct).** "Everyone added AI capture. Nobody closes the loop." No tool combined Windows context capture, untangling mixed German and English dumps, and duties from booking to the next due date. Substitutes named: Notion with Custom Agents, chat assistants with memory ("you have to remember to ask, which is the step that fails"), Todoist with Ramble, Mem. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)

## Capture

- **One capture point, saved at once, no questions (proposed 10 Oct; carried through).** "A hotkey opens the intake on the PC; share, widget or voice on the phone. Saved instantly, with no decisions." [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz). "Nothing is asked first." [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe). "Add has no extra rows or questions." [#18](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/18)
- **Add is one line of text (decided, ADR-0052).** Found words, defaults and changes stay in the text. [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). Enter saves; Shift+Enter new line; Esc cancels. [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71)
- **The same box everywhere (decided, ADR-0034, 15 Oct).** "Ctrl Space opens the same box everywhere. Type, Enter." [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E). Later artifacts use Ctrl+Space or Ctrl+Alt+Space over any app, taking selected text. [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4), [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71)
- **One line as the only door (proposed, ADR-0049).** Direction A "One Line" recommended over B Sittings, C Promises and D Sift Notices: "the same Add on every door, Claude's findings as proposals on the saved task, and follow-through as a state of the task. You learn nothing new, and there is no inbox, sitting, ledger or offers line to keep up." Mean judge scores: A 76.2, D 74.0, C 71.2, B 69.9; an external review by three models also put A first and D second. A was to take nine pieces from B, C and D: a fading one-line receipt, an action row that follows the rung, a call-sheet layout, "Anything promised?" when an appointment ends, a neutral line after days away, replies pause a wait only from a known address, number or thread, health states such as "Mail watch stopped", at most three invitations a day, and finding a number by rules when Claude is off. [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Receipt after saving (decided in issues).** A one-line receipt shows whether the capture is still pending. [#18](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/18). In CAP the receipt fades after a few seconds and was to be an always-on-top window that takes no focus (untested). [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Capture is never lost (decided in issues).** A capture is written to durable browser storage before the screen shows a pending receipt, then retried with its idempotency key; the documented limit is that clearing site data loses pending items. Reason: "Nothing may be lost when the server is unreachable." [#4](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/4). Save-to-confirmation p95 under two seconds with no model call in the path. [#18](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/18). CASE set a gate of hotkey-to-saved ≤ 2 s and zero lost captures over two weeks. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Found words (decided, F05 accepted).** Dates, deadlines, repeats, projects, people and places are outlined in their kind's colour as the user types; defaults sit at the end; a changed word shows the original struck through with Restore. A table of at least 40 German and English phrases must pass by rules, with no model. [#19](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/19). "One kind = one colour." [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). Reason for rules: the date grammar passed 273 of 277 of the user's own phrases, so "Dates need no model". [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **No syntax required (proposed).** German and English are read alike; optional prefixes such as `werkstatt:` and `uns:`. Reason: "75 / 249 titles have no verb". [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe). Optional capture words (F07) were still open as Q18. [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Dates found in other people's text are offers, not set (proposed).** "Other people's messages mention dates that aren't your deadlines." [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71), [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2). Only confident dates set reminders, because date-finding "still makes about 2 mistakes per 100 notes". [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), experiment PA-10 in [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Pasting several lines (decided in issues).** Several lines stay one task with a "Split into N tasks" button; a first line ending in a colon makes steps; a pasted list becomes tasks; a pasted link shows title and domain; files and photos can be attached. [#20](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/20). TODO had proposed "Several lines become several tasks after a preview". [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **Original text kept (decided).** "Your words stay exactly as typed. Cleaned versions sit next to them." [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz). The original text of each capture is stored unchanged beside what rules or the model made of it. [#16](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/16). Reason: dump splitting failed (OR-03, F1 0.55–0.69), so "Keep originals; splits are suggestions". [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Context at capture (Sift era, measured).** App, window, Chrome address, mic and calendar recorded at capture; private windows left out; grabbing it took 38–44 ms p95 (WX-12). [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz). Later decided: "location and window titles stay on the device". [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY). In PLAN, device context (F117–F120: privacy controls, source context, activity buffer, browser extension) sits in milestone D2. [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Phone capture.** Quick-settings tile, widget, share sheet ("Share to Sift"), working offline. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). An iPhone web app has no share sheet ("WebKit bug 194593 is still open"); "A Shortcut could add one later (unverified)." [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2), [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71). An external review added "A one-tap mic on the widget, with no auto-save timer". [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Kept out of screen shares (proposed).** Add, the receipt and the window stay out of screen captures by default (WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE; Teams untested), with "Safe to show" to let one in. [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2). "Hidden from screen shares; work and private kept apart." [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E)
- **Capture from email.** "Found automatically, added by you": the model proposes tasks and nothing is added without a tap. Reason: "the model found 92% of the tasks, but 3 in 10 of its suggestions weren't tasks." "Mail never gives orders." Work mail stays off. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe). An Inbox entry "reads as if typed into Add". [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). Gmail and the Inbox were milestone C1 ("Mail to tasks was asked for on 2026-10-15"). [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A), [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md)
- **Capture by agents (milestone C5).** MCP and a CLI for local agents. [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md). CASE had proposed a read-only MCP. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Earlier UI-round capture options (Sift notes era).** Round 1 options: "Thread — message it like Telegram" (risk: "the thread becomes its own long list") and "Palette — summoned by hotkey" (risk: "out of sight, out of mind"). Round 5: "Writing asks nothing." Round 9 proposed three modes (Capture, about 90% of input; Page; Canvas). [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E). Chat as the main screen was left out: "I do not like the pure chat messenger style." [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)

## Views

- **Now: only today (decided in issues; ADR-0084, 0086–0090).** Now shows the day, the planning card when one is waiting, and one Today list of what the user committed and what is due. The lead item is the first of: a deadline or appointment today, a recurring item whose booking window is open, the first committed task. [#22](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/22), [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). Reason: "Having a too long list just feels demotivating." [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **No overdue count, red, streak or badge (decided).** A UI test must find none in Now. [#22](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/22). Reason: "18 / 18 open tasks with a due date are past due" in the user's Notion; "Long lists stress you; gamification fades". [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **Order stays put (decided).** The list order does not change until the user navigates away or reloads; a completed task stays crossed out until the user leaves. [#22](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/22). "Order stays put while you look." [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71). Ticking is "one motion in place, no toast". [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Planning the day (decided in issues).** Planning starts with everything due ticked; removing a due item takes it off today only for that day and keeps its date. Work days come from configurable work days and hours; every other day is a free day. The planning card uses rules only at first. [#22](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/22). On a free day, proposals are private projects only, each project's next step first (ADR-0084). [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Earlier Now layout (proposed 15–16 Oct).** Lead item, Today, "Next · one step per active project", a From email fold, Waiting on, Coming up. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71). By 20 October Next moved into its own view (next entry).
- **Next: opened on purpose (decided in issues; ADR-0088, 0089).** A Next view that "opens only when the user chooses it, and shows no popup or notification": Unblocked first, then Waiting on (who, expected when, what it blocks), the next 7 days and open booking windows, and each active project's next step. Reason: "The user needs a place to look ahead that never prompts the user." [#26](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/26), [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Waiting on is a view, not a list to review (proposed).** [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Task view: annotated words (option chosen for polishing).** Four rounds from four models; round 4 had three models polish the same design, Gemini 4 Argon's "Annotated Words", "to make it calmer, cleaner and more finished". Other ideas shown: the task as a sentence, a timeline, focus planes, quiet signals, margin notes and others. No decision is recorded on the page. [TASK](https://claude.ai/artifact/6eUDZnwXWFofwu1hu6njQ2). MOCK's task view references "round 4, the polished Annotated Words". [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Open task (decided in issues).** Shows text, description, links, attachments, steps, source and history, with Date and Repeat rows always visible and quick picks for Today, Tomorrow, Friday and Next week. Reason: "A task needs its details without cluttering Add." [#21](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/21)
- **One action per task (decided, ADR-0090).** Open (a saved link), Search (finding something) or Order (a saved shop link); pressing one also puts the task on today and starts the focus; never more than one labelled button, and none sends, buys or pays. Reason: "A task should open where the work happens, without needing mail or contacts." [#24](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/24), [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Projects page (decided in issues).** Each project shows one state line (done and open counts, next step, waiting) and no percentage or health colour. Reason: "Bigger topics need a place that stays calm." [#27](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/27)
- **Recurring view (decided in issues).** Grouped Needs you, Booked, then due months with a Later fold. [#32](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/32)
- **Lists view (decided in issues; redesign suggested).** A calm index grouped Shops, Online and Other with counts, an empty-lists fold, find and New. [#34](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/34). The 19 October redesign suggestion (not decided): Fable's calm index as base, because "it is the cleanest and keeps every list on one phone screen", plus Opus's Today errand card ("the errand is the one list that matters now"), its in-shop phone list and Astra's item search; tiles and item previews left out because "Both add weight to every row for a gain that search and the Today card already give." [LISTS](https://claude.ai/artifact/2QNgcoGEYoTQnsMYi2S3TQ)
- **Search (decided in issues).** Full text over task text, descriptions, links and steps, including done and trashed items; from a result the user can add the task to today "with no limit on how many". [#35](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/35). Search "takes a typed line as a new task" (ADR-0084). [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). Sift-era search: "your exact words… No chat answer." [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E)
- **Earlier UI directions (Sift notes era, options and round-9 decisions).** Round 1: Panel (always visible on the small screen below the main monitor), Thread, Palette, Desk. Round 2 principles from the user's review of 11 October: "visual, not walls of text"; "calm and non-technical"; "no chat style"; "the raw input is always kept". Round 9 decided "direction A", "organized pages built from their sources", "your own pages, which Sift never rearranges" and "drawings in Sift". [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E). These page and drawing decisions were later left out of scope (ADR-0055). [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Meeting packs (Sift era, proposed).** Going into a meeting, 3–5 relevant items, "never a dump. Ignored items fade out." Reason: success criterion S2. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **App shell (decided in issues).** Installable web app on desktop and Android Chrome and Edge; its own screens for offline, server down and signed out; Settings opens on an overview with groups; time zone drives today and reminders; add, complete, undo and search reachable without a mouse; automated accessibility check. [#17](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/17)
- **Visual design (decided, still in the repo).** Warm paper, soft ink in four strengths, "Nothing is pure black or pure white"; four accents (Slate default, Sage, Clay, Dusk) where only the accent changes; fixed colours per kind of found word; Geist typeface; light mode only so far, dark and high contrast to be added. Rules: components use tokens, never literal colours. [TOKENS](../design/README.md). MOCK also offered System, Light, Dark and High contrast appearance. ## Structure

- **Plain date, deadline and time are different (decided in issues).** "A plain date puts the task in Today on that date and does not add it earlier; a deadline is a separate hard mark; a time makes an appointment." [#21](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/21). A plain date "never rings and never turns red". [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71). Reason for dates only on real deadlines: all 18 open dated Notion tasks were past due. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **No priority, context, tag or snooze fields (proposed, carried into the issues by omission).** Reason: "232 / 240 priorities were left at the default 'Medium'. Contexts, snooze, wait date and recurrence were used 0 times." Kanban left out: "two status fields disagreed on 44% of tasks". Location reminders left out: "Coarse and unreliable; Google dropped them". [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **Duties with lead time and booking (decided in issues).** A recurring task repeats on a fixed schedule or an interval after it is done; the next three dates show; lead time opens a booking window; "Booked 14.11. 10:00" is understood; "Roji never books anything". Edge rules: a repeat on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months; a local time keeps its wall-clock time across daylight-saving changes. Reason: "Duties that repeat must never be lost or guessed." [#32](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/32). Lifecycle in TODO: Resting → Window open → Booked → Done → Next due; a duty "never completes or cancels itself through silence". Reason: the car inspection was missed two years running, real duties lived in notes ("4 / 251 tasks are recurring"), and "No product we reviewed takes one from 'window open' through booking." [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Changing a recurring task's date (decided in issues).** Applies to this occurrence by default with "all" as a choice; history records which. [#21](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/21)
- **Waiting, "after", deferring and Someday (decided in issues).** A task can be marked waiting with a check-back date and who or what it waits for, and stays out of Today until then. A task can wait for another task ("after"); when that is done or the check-back date arrives, it appears in Next as Unblocked. A deferred task is silent until its date. A task set aside twice with "not now", or one in a paused project, goes to Someday. Reason: "Tasks that cannot move yet must stay out of the way and come back by themselves." [#25](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/25)
- **Follow-through as a state of the task (proposed, ADR-0049).** An outreach moves through To reach, With them, Promised, Reply?, Paused, Quiet, Decision. "Only Got it, Drop and Hand over close a wait … Silence, counts and passed dates close nothing." [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2). Follow-through was placed in W2, "Features that add little before you have used W1". [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Projects (decided in issues).** A project has an outcome line; areas group projects and are set in Settings (rename, order, a Now rule); moving a task keeps its details and history. [#27](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/27). TODO had proposed "A project is just a name": no setup form, no dates. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **Phases, steps and make-it-a-project (decided in issues).** Optional ordered phases with target dates (an empty phase disappears); checkable steps with optional dates; one click turns a task into a project or a phase into a project, offered once at about seven steps. Reason: "Large work needs optional structure that grows on demand." [#28](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/28)
- **A project's next step (decided in issues).** Its first open task that is not waiting, deferred or blocked. [#26](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/26)
- **Inactive projects sink (decided in issues).** No change for 30 days and a project leaves Now by itself; its deadlines and recurring items still appear. Pausing moves a project to Someday; marking a project done with open tasks asks what to do with them. Reason: "Quiet projects must leave the user's view without losing any duty." [#29](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/29)
- **Place lists (decided in issues).** A task or step can be linked to a place (a shop, an online shop, or just a list); each list gathers its items across projects; ticking an item completes it everywhere. [#34](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/34). Recurring list items (ADR-0092): "on its list only when due, one open at a time". [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Project templates (decided, ADR-0091; W2).** "A template is a project with a template mark; using one copies it, nothing syncs back." [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4), [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **History, undo, trash (decided in issues).** History is the change log, never compacted, showing person, device and time; undo for completing, dating, moving and deleting; trash for 30 days. Reason: "Nothing the user enters should be lost, and any change should be undoable." [#16](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/16). Undo "makes the opposite change and writes it down too; older states are not restored" (Q116). [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Simultaneous edits (decided).** Different fields merge; the same field changed twice keeps both versions and shows "Keep mine · Keep theirs · Keep both". [#4](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/4). "When two people change the same thing, both versions are kept and you choose." [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Spaces and sharing in the data model (decided); sharing screens deferred (decided).** A space is the tenant boundary; one deployment hosts many households or teams; shares only through explicit grants. [#2](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/2). "Sharing and extra users wait (ADR-0101)… The data model keeps spaces and the sharing rule." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). Earlier proposals: Mine and Ours, with Ours shared with his wife, because "What she tells you goes straight into Ours … That's the path where the car inspection got lost" [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe); groups and roles Admin, Owner, Manager, Member ("As in Google Groups"), and "Nobody, admins included, sees another person's private tasks" [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71), [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4). Sharing a to-do shares its linked mails (ADR-0074). [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Sync-ready writes (decided in issues).** Every write is a named, versioned command with a caller-made id, idempotency key, actor and device, a per-client counter, and time and time zone; an append-only change log with a per-space sequence; tombstones kept 90 days. Reason: "Native clients will sync later, so the web app must already write changes the way a sync needs them, or the server data model will need rework." [#3](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/3)
- **Sift-era item model (proposed, 10 Oct).** Captures stored verbatim and never changed; items point at the exact words they came from; every value records who set it (you, a rule or Claude) and why; typed items such as Duty, Snippet, Task, Idea. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)

## AI

- **Rules first, the model adds proposals (decided).** "Models are called by the server through a configurable gateway or provider API key. Rules run first; the model adds proposals." [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md). "Rules and code first. The model only where it adds value." [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Propose and confirm, never silent filing (decided by 11 Oct, ADR-0018).** Reason: "no signal reaches ≤ 2% misfiling; same answer on repeat only 50%" (OR-04, OR-11), and placement accuracy was 62% with Haiku using area profiles. "Filing became propose and confirm." [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz), [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E). "Dashed means proposed. Until you keep it, a proposal doesn't file, share or ring." No confidence gate for places and projects, because confidence bands were 20%, 46%, 35% and 65% right (OR-04). [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Proposals after each save (decided in issues).** Rules propose a duplicate (same text ignoring case and punctuation, within edit distance 2, among open tasks and those done in the last 7 days), a place (a known shop word) and a split; the model may add a wait, a project or others. One tap to accept or remove; an ignored proposal hides after 14 days; "nothing changes the task without the user's tap"; "a slow or failed model never delays saving". [#31](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/31)
- **Breakdown on request (decided in issues).** On the user's tap the model proposes 3 to 7 steps or a few phases; the user unticks what they don't want; code rejects invented dates or people; a proposal never overwrites text. [#30](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/30)
- **Everything works with the model off (decided).** "Capture and every view work with all model features switched off; original text is always kept." [#5](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/5). With all switches off Roji says "not sorted yet" and nothing breaks; a provider failure shows in the receipt and never blocks saving. [#15](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/15). At a monthly limit "the assistant pauses… Tasks and reminders keep working." [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Provider settings (decided in issues).** The user chooses a model gateway address and key or a provider API key, and the model per task; a small inexpensive model is the default; the key is never shown after saving; each model feature has its own switch; every run is logged with cost and a per-day limit. [#15](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/15). "Any provider, signed in or with an API key, for everyone or just you, and a model per task (his words, 2026-10-18)": Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, OmniRoute or a local model. [MOCK](https://claude.ai/artifact/5qrMiQ5jY8bxWPKhRrf5D4)
- **Which model and route.** CASE proposed Claude Haiku 5.5 by default, called directly from the server, because the direct API "enables caching, strict outputs and batches, which the router hid" (left as "your call"). [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz). PLAN (W1, F110): "Haiku 5.5 through OmniRoute … Server calls OmniRoute with your API key." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). OmniRoute versus a direct key was open as Q79 on 16 October. [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Device or cloud models (decided 17–18 Oct; deferred to native apps).** "Each person picks Device models only or Cloud models when online." [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY). F111 "Needs the native clients: D1, then A1 (ADR-0095)." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Privacy contract (decided in issues).** A written contract lists, per model feature, the fields sent, never sent, and the switch; runs logged with masked input, output, cost and outcome; a per-person budget; retention stated; deletion after the 30-day window removes or anonymizes data in logs, files and derived outputs. Reason: "A cloud model sees personal text, so the rules for what leaves the server, what is kept and what deletion removes must exist before the features that use them." [#5](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/5)
- **Masking and injection safety (measured; proposed).** Closed-choice schemas checked by code: 0 of 48 planted injections changed a placement (OR-10). Secrets masked before any call; rules caught 98–100% of known secret patterns but 65–71% of new phrasings, so "Needs a secrets decision before captures reach a model by default"; the options left open were moving secrets to Vaultwarden or keeping them encrypted. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz), [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Model used for email suggestions only (proposed 15 Oct).** "Batched and masked. Everything else is rules." [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe)
- **No assistant persona, no written sentences (Sift notes era).** "No assistant persona. Sift prepares; you send, book and buy." "Everything on the page is someone's exact words… Sift writes no sentences." [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E). "Draft, for you to send yourself. Sift never sends it." [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71). "Sift never sends, calls or books." [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Costs (measured, 10 and 16 Oct).** $0.64 per 1,000 captures placed; about 60 cents a month at 30 captures a day; 2.7 s p95 from capture to a proposed place. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz). About $0.19–0.58 a month. [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Untested model features at 20 Oct.** F40 (first model feature, OR-16), F59 (OR-17) and F113 (after-save proposals, OR-18) are marked untested. [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)

## Motivation

- **Done moment: stones on a path (decided, F24 accepted).** Each completed task adds a stone to the path in Today's header with a short animation and a sound; finishing the day plays a second sound; sounds only when the Sounds switch is on; with reduced motion the stone appears at once. Reason: "The user picks what to do now and gets a small reward for finishing." [#23](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/23). Chosen from seven options (Ink, Day ring, Collect, Stepping stones, Warm light, Chime; then "Stones that fly, with sound"); the canvas gives no reason for the pick. Shared rules: rows have a fixed height "so nothing moves while he looks"; finish line "Today's list is done. / Anything else is extra."; "sound only ever plays in answer to a click". [DONE](https://claude.ai/artifact/Fs6ALZ3QkY3YPGosoJdPAy). Motion timings in [TOKENS](../design/tokens.json) (`motion`).
- **No streaks, red or guilt (decided).** See Views. "No red piles, streaks or guilt. Old items expire quietly." [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Check-in (proposed; W2).** At most five cards, one tap each, early evening Mon–Thu and Friday morning; "Skipping it costs nothing"; a card skipped twice retires and its task moves to Someday; card types include date at risk, duty window open, from email, next step unchanged for 3 weeks, waiting past its check-back date; a stuck step offers a smaller step. Reasons cited: Nordby 2022 (plain repeated reminders didn't help) and Fitz 2019 (batched prompts at a natural break did better), noted as "untested" in an app. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), [PROTO](https://claude.ai/artifact/RBZyMLmCBVZsBXWrFWUd71). Placed in W2. [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Day and week goals (options; idea at 20 Oct).** Three directions: inside the check-ins and Now ("the least new UI"); a planning moment of its own ("Plan tomorrow", with work days versus free days); the week as the frame ("Choose up to 3. A small part of the week is enough."). Shared copy: "Nothing chosen. Your day is still here." [GOALS](https://claude.ai/artifact/9p9FcX6cMNC88hqMq4L5pw). "Week goals are still only an idea" (F26). [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Prompts and reminders limited (decided in issues).** Quiet hours and one daily budget apply to suggestions; appointments and deadlines always remind and sit outside the budget. [#33](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/33). "Only four kinds ring": a duty window, an appointment the evening before, a hard deadline, the check-in; at most three a day. Reason given in CASE: in a clinical reminder system each extra reminder cut acceptance by 30%, and ChatGPT's daily Pulse feed was retired in 2026. [TODO](https://claude.ai/artifact/6Lb4w7HJc1XrVRKzZtCxJe), [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Survives being ignored (proposed).** "It keeps working when you ignore it for a week." Reason: "Mental-health apps keep 3.9% of users after 15 days"; "Capture keeps flowing when ignored. Nothing needs daily upkeep." A neutral line after days away. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz), [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2)
- **Work and private timing (proposed).** Calls to trades on Friday 9–12 by default; work items lead Now without a ring, "never in the evening or on Friday". [CAP](https://claude.ai/artifact/95Qj76afqYruwC6kwBkMk2). "Personal things wait for your own time." [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E)
- **Weekly review.** CASE's principles call for no daily upkeep; UIP round 9 lists "weekly review" among items unchanged since round 5 but not drawn. No source in this set defines it further. [UIP](https://claude.ai/artifact/GpkpbNDsECWaMjJHqFHL2E)

## Operations

- **Self-hosted on the user's Proxmox, private (decided 17–18 Oct).** Behind his existing reverse proxy, Let's Encrypt via Cloudflare DNS-01, Tailscale when away; "Nothing about Sift is reachable from the internet." [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY). F97: "Self-hosted at the roji address over Tailscale; nothing public." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). The README framed it as "Runs behind a reverse proxy, on a private network or hosted." [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md)
- **Staging and production from one image (decided).** Two VMs; staging has synthetic data, connectors and push off, its own low-limit model key; every merge to `main` deploys to staging; production is promoted by hand; a failed deploy leaves the previous version running. [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md), [#12](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/12), [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). Reason: "Every build needs a real place to be tried before it reaches production." [#12](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/12)
- **Promotion and migrations (decided in issues).** One immutable artifact with a migration manifest; forward-only, expand-then-contract migrations; a destructive migration needs a verified backup first; rehearsals from each released schema. Reason: "A bad migration or deploy must be recoverable." [#6](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/6)
- **Backups (decided for now).** "Backups are handled by the deployment platform; the operator chooses the tooling." [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md). Proxmox backup "Your decision for now. Off-host copy and an application-level restore test stay open (Q95)." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). An isolated restore procedure: network blocked before first boot, workers off, secrets replaced, disposed afterwards. Reason: a restore "must not bring real credentials or outbound effects into a development environment." [#7](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/7)
- **Sign-in (decided, ADR-0059).** Passkey or password (Argon2id); one session per device with remote sign-out; a first-run page creates the owner account, the space and the model setting. [#13](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/13), [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Export and deletion (decided in issues).** Markdown export and a lossless, re-importable JSON export with attachments; deleting the account offers an export first and removes data after 30 days unless cancelled. Reason: "The user's data must be portable and deletable." [#36](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/36)
- **Synthetic data on staging (decided).** A generator for German and English phrases and edge-case dates, and an end-to-end day test. [#37](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/37). Production reference data: "Your idea: start synthetic, add this later. Privacy rules to be set first." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A). "Your real data stays in sift-data", not in the repo. [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Analytics.** "Events are collected from day one (F99)." [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Built by coding agents; the issue is the spec (decided 18 Oct).** AI-drafted GitHub issues checked by the user; no spec files, task files or per-feature folders. User's reason: "I don't want verbose spec files … It shouldn't feel like the process bleeds into the source code." [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY), [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md). Build loop (proposed): issue → one Orca worktree per issue → agent implements → review → one PR at a time → staging, then production → use produces new issues. [PLAN](https://claude.ai/artifact/GcGmpVcEWDKaYbgm98ez8A)
- **Public repo, MIT, one PR at a time (decided).** A public repo on the personal account, MIT licence (ADR-0071); an agent merges one PR at a time because "There is no GitHub merge queue without an organization"; GitHub-hosted runners only, because on a self-hosted runner "strangers' pull requests could run code on it". [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY), [README](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/README.md). CASE had flagged that "fully AI-written code may carry thin copyright" and proposed a lawyer confirm the licence. [CASE](https://claude.ai/artifact/VYiuun5AtKfLvSphFWZRJz)
- **Guardrails (decided in issues).** `main` requires a PR and passing checks, dismisses stale approvals, forbids force pushes, including for admins; `CODEOWNERS` covers workflows, checks and contracts; a PR that weakens a check fails until the user approves; PR jobs have no deployment credentials. Reason: "the rules agents follow must be enforced and their remaining gaps visible." [#8](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/8), [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Agent-friendly code layout (decided in the scaffold, since removed).** One check command run by CI; crate boundaries checked by tests (I/O-free `domain` and `parse`, `store` owns the database, `server` depends on the rest); "one change, one module"; new web packages wait three days before install; no secrets (gitleaks); a per-worktree dev environment with its own ports and database. [AGENTS](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/blob/2dcd301/AGENTS.md), [#38](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/pull/38), [#39](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/39). Reasons in ARCH: "Agents fail on breadth", "Context is a budget", "Agents game checks they can edit", "Shared files make agents collide". [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY). An experiment was to measure whether the Rust check loop was fast enough for several agents at once. [#10](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/10)
- **Development environment (decided).** Unix: Linux, or Windows through WSL; agents run with no secrets. [#1](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/issues/1), [ARCH](https://claude.ai/artifact/DKF2joZPdTBBGNrV12QfTY)
- **Restart (decided 24 Nov).** "Planning restarts from the product, so the Rust workspace, the web app, the boundary tool and the dev scripts go. The design tokens stay." `scripts/check` keeps only the secret scan and its tests, "which keeps the required CI check meaningful". [commit 05ee454](https://github.com/piemkay/roji/commit/05ee454)
